# Code of conduct

Church Slides is made for churches, by people from many churches and none.
Treat everyone here the way you'd want to be treated at the door on a Sunday.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- No arguments about theology or denominations. Every tradition that sings is
  welcome to use the app, and we don't take sides.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't post anyone's personal details, or
  recordings of real services without the church's permission.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer at martinmain93@gmail.com. Reports
are kept confidential. A maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or ban
anyone who breaks this code, in this repository and its discussions.
