Church Slides next-line phone models

Two small phone-recognition networks, made for Church Slides: one (5.0
million parameters) reads the audio encoder output of Qwen3-ASR 0.6B, the
other (4.4 million parameters) reads Whisper small.en's. Their weights are
our own work and ship with the Apple-silicon engine.

They were trained only on public music: songs from the Free Music Archive
dataset (FMA: A Dataset for Music Analysis, Defferrard, Benzi, Vandergheynst
and Bresson, ISMIR 2017; https://github.com/mdeff/fma), with lyrics
transcribed automatically by Whisper. No church recordings were used.

Each FMA track is published by its artist under the licence the artist chose,
mostly Creative Commons licences, some of them non-commercial. The models hold
no audio or lyrics from those tracks: only learned weights. Church Slides is
free of charge.
