Church Slides next-line phone model

A small phone-recognition network (4.4 million parameters) that reads the
Whisper small.en encoder's output, made for Church Slides. Its weights are our
own work and ship with the Apple-silicon engine.

It was trained only on public music: songs from the Free Music Archive
dataset (FMA: A Dataset for Music Analysis, Defferrard, Benzi, Vandergheynst
and Bresson, ISMIR 2017; https://github.com/mdeff/fma), with lyrics
transcribed automatically by Whisper. No church recordings were used.

Each FMA track is published by its artist under the licence the artist chose,
mostly Creative Commons licences, some of them non-commercial. The model holds
no audio or lyrics from those tracks: only learned weights. Church Slides is
free of charge.
